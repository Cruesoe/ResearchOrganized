[b]Sorts research into tech-level tabs and lays each tab out as a tidy, readable tree.[/b]

In some cases the result is not strictly linear, but I believe it makes the tree much more readable in highly modded games.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Technologies are routed to their tech level's tab: Primitive, Neolithic, Medieval, Industrial, High Industrial, Late Industrial, Spacer, Ultra and Archotech. Empty tabs are hidden.
[*]Industrial is split three ways: Industrial, High Industrial (needs the Hi-Tech Research Bench) and Late Industrial (needs the Multi-Analyzer).
[*]Entity knowledge technologies are moved to the Anomaly tab.
[*]No overlapping nodes and fewer crossing lines. Follow-ups sit to the right of what they need, and branches fill the space beside their prerequisites instead of trailing off the end of the tab.
[*]A technology is raised to the era of its prerequisites, so a follow-up never sits in an earlier era than what it needs. Its research cost changes to match.
[*]Nodes are colored by era and shaded by status (unavailable, available, researched).
[*]Tab tooltips show completed projects and research points, the research window remembers where you scrolled, and a gear button opens the settings.
[*]Schematics can teach technologies from every era tab.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hide Unavailable Projects[/b]: shows only finished projects and those you can start now. Off by default.
[*][b]Hide Future Tech Levels[/b]: hides unfinished projects above your colony's tech level. Off by default.
[*][b]Full Screen Research Window[/b]: makes the research window as tall as the screen and fits as many projects into each column as that allows. A button beside the gear in the research window toggles it too. Off by default.
[*][b]Show Tab Project Counts[/b]: shows completed/total counts on each tab.
[*][b]Combine All Tabs[/b]: puts everything except Anomaly, Gravship and Save Our Ship 2 on the Main tab, with each era in its own block from left to right. Off by default.
[*][b]Combine Industrial[/b]: merges High and Late Industrial back into one Industrial tab. Off by default.
[*][b]Color Cards by Tech Level[/b]: pick each era's color, or turn the colors off.
[*][b]Suppress Emergence Lines[/b]: hides lines to Node Research's emergence nodes unless one end is selected. On by default.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with [b]Node Research[/b] (foundation technologies get a gold corner mark, and you can switch freely between its node view and these tabs), [b]Semi Random Research Pfork Continued[/b] (its button and the settings gear sit side by side), [b]Research Icons[/b] (columns widen to fit its wider cards), [b]Save Our Ship 2[/b] (its Ship and Archotech tabs keep their projects), and [b]ProgressionLESS[/b] and [b]VFE Tribals[/b] (their research lands in the right tabs).
Tested with Ferny's Progression mod pack and Path of the Muffalo (as long as you remove the conflicts!).
Not compatible with other research tab organizers, because they control the same system.
Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
