This mod sorts research into the right tech level tabs and organizes the tree.
In some cases the result is not strictly linear, but I believe it makes the tree much more readable in highly modded games.

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Era-Specific Tabs:[/b] Technologies are automatically routed to their tech level tabs: Primitive, Neolithic, Medieval, Industrial, Spacer, Ultra and Archotech. Empty tabs are hidden.
[*][b]Single Tab View:[/b] Prefer one tree? Combine All Tabs puts everything except Anomaly and Gravship on the Main tab, with each era in its own block from left to right. (Optional!)
[*][b]The Industrial Split:[/b] The Industrial era is divided into three tabs: Industrial, High Industrial (requires the Hi-Tech Research Bench) and Late Industrial (requires the Multi-Analyzer). Combine Industrial merges them back into one. (Optional!)
[*][b]Anomaly Integration:[/b] Entity knowledge technologies are moved to the Anomaly tab.
[*][b]Clean Layout Engine:[/b] No overlapping nodes and fewer crossing lines. Follow-ups sit to the right of what they need, big hubs stand out on their own, and branches fill the space beside their prerequisites instead of trailing off the end of the tab.
[*][b]Era Consistency:[/b] A technology is raised to the era of its prerequisites, so a follow-up never sits in an earlier era than what it needs. Its research cost changes to match.
[*][b]Dynamic Tech Coloring:[/b] Nodes are colored by era and shaded by status (Unavailable, Available, Researched).
[*][b]Choose Your Colors:[/b] Don't like the colors I chose? Change them for each tab, or turn them off.
[*][b]Handy Extras:[/b] Tab tooltips show completed projects and research points, the research window remembers where you scrolled, and a gear button opens the settings.
[/list]

[h2]Works With[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Node Research:[/b] foundation technologies get a gold corner mark, and you can switch freely between its node view and these tabs.
[*][b]Semi Random Research Pfork Continued:[/b] its button and the settings gear sit side by side.
[*][b]Research Icons[/b]
[*][b]ProgressionLESS[/b] and [b]VFE Tribals[/b]: their research lands in the right tabs.
[/list]

[list]
[*]Safe to add to or remove from existing save files.
[*]Built to organize heavily modded loadouts automatically.

[/list]
